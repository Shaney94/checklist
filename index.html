Cleaner FAQ

Common questions about payment, supplies and cleaning operations

Airbnb processes your cleaning payment on the day the guest checks in. Please contact me and I’ll invite you as a co-host. Once you accept the invite and add your payout details, the payment can take up to 2 days to clear.
Regular supplies such as coffee, toilet rolls, kitchen rolls and other consumables are delivered directly to your address. We use Amazon Subscribe & Save for recurring deliveries, so please keep an eye out for parcels.

Please message me as soon as you notice anything running low, even if there is still enough for the current clean. This gives time to reorder before the next guest.
Take clear photos before cleaning or moving anything, then report the issue to me. Pay particular attention to the TV, speakers, monitors, coffee machine, beds, lights and other key appliances.
Put the item into the designated storage area and tell me what you found. If possible, include a photo so I can contact the guest quickly.

Start the laundry as soon as you arrive because it takes the longest. Laundry capsules are kept above the microwave. The washing machine is in the kitchen, and laundry can be dried downstairs at Temple Laundrette if required.
Important: always click the booking event in the Hostex calendar to open the full reservation details and confirm the actual checkout date. The date shown on the calendar tile can be misleading at a glance. For example, an event may appear under the 6th even though the guest actually checks out on the 7th. The clean is due after the guest checks out, so always open the event and verify the checkout date before planning the clean.

Take clear photos and/or video of the entire property once the clean is complete, including the kitchen, bathroom, living room, bedrooms and any relevant details. This protects you if a guest later raises a cleanliness complaint or another issue, because there is a clear record of the condition in which you left the property. Any cleanliness issue reported after the clean could otherwise come back to you, so please make sure the final condition is always documented.
Deep cleans should be completed as a solo 4-hour clean. The purpose is not only to clean more thoroughly, but also to give one person dedicated time every three months to inspect the property properly and deal with anything that may have changed. From time to time, new electrical items or important equipment may also have been delivered — for example a washing machine or another appliance — and the quarterly deep clean is the opportunity to make sure everything is present, correctly positioned, undamaged and working as expected. Please do not rush or split the deep clean between multiple cleaners unless agreed in advance.
